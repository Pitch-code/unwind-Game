## Full description
Skein is a quiet puzzle about undoing knots. Each level hands you a tangle of
pins joined by ropes. Drag the pins around until no rope crosses another and the
board settles — that's it. No timer, no score, no pressure. Just the small,
satisfying click of a knot coming undone.

Every level you solve grows a plant in your zen garden. Over time a whole garden
takes shape beneath your hands, so there's always something easing gently toward
completion.

- Hundreds of hand-tuned levels, every one guaranteed solvable.
- Starts easy — the first levels are a pin or two out of place — and grows with
  you at its own calm pace.
- A living zen garden that blooms as you play.
- Tangled ropes glow so you always know what's left to sort.
- Play offline, anywhere. No account, no sign-in.

One optional purchase removes ads and unlocks every garden theme. That's the
only purchase — no subscriptions, no coins, no energy timers.

Sit back, breathe, and unwind.
